Guiness

Guiness is a private personal assistant used only by its owner, Artur Vlasov. It is not a public service and does not accept other users.

With the owner's consent it reads and organizes his own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Contacts, prepares drafts, and keeps his notes, tasks and expense records. Anything sent in the owner's name (email, sharing, invitations) is done only after he approves it.
